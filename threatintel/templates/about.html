About

This app combines CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog with CVSS scoring from the NIST National Vulnerability Database, joined on CVE ID.

Anyone can browse the catalog. Signed-in users can load NVD details for a CVE, keep a watchlist with private notes, and refresh the KEV feed.
